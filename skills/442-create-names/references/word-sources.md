# Word sources

Lists to work, not lists to copy. Two minutes per list, three or four lists per
pass, and write down every word without judging. Coincidence is the goal.

## The lists

**Natural world.** Weather words (squall, haar, thaw, drizzle), tides, minerals,
bird behaviour (murmuration, roost, lek), bark, fungal forms, seafloor features,
regional wind names (mistral, sirocco, chinook), snow words, cloud genera.

**Tools and trades.** Cooper, thatcher, wheelwright, farrier, chandlery, foundry,
kiln, lathe, template, plumb, flange, gasket, sift, sieve, trowel, anvil, spindle,
shuttle, warp, weft, bobbin, awl, rasp, drift, punch, die, jig.

**Kitchen and table.** Mise, roux, proof, temper, cure, render, steep, skim,
baste, fold, wheel, larder, pantry, cellar, crock, gourd, peel, zester, mortar,
colander, trivet, coupe.

**Card and board games.** Trump, dummy, meld, draw, deal, ante, bluff, suit, rook,
pawn, bishop, gambit, tempo, crib, skat, tarot.

**Instruments and notation.** Clef, fret, bridge, mute, reed, bow, rest, staff,
timbre, cadence, decibel, chord, drone, bellows, chime, tonic.

**Aviation and space.** Lift, drag, chord, stall, yaw, glide, flare, vector,
apron, gantry, perigee, escape, canopy, ballast, trim.

**Water and shipping.** Keel, hull, gunwale, lee, wake, sluice, sloop, scow,
barge, moor, dredge, buoy, anchor, sextant, fathom, cargo, draught.

**Textiles and knots.** Plait, splice, hem, selvage, twill, worsted, reef, hitch,
bight, grommet, warp.

**Medicine and anatomy.** Suture, pulse, cornea, marrow, sinew, valve, cortex,
tendon, graft, triage.

**Architecture and civic.** Lintel, arch, arcade, transept, cloister, plinth,
sill, cornice, quoin, cairn, mullion.

**Greek and Latin roots.** Raw material, never a scheme. `hapt-` touch, `stath-`
standing, `rhiz-` root, `kine-` motion, `paus-` stop, `troph-` nourishment, `luc-`
light, `somn-` sleep.

**Mythology and folklore.** Minor figures beat gods. Household spirits (brownie,
domovoi, lares), threshold guardians, tricksters, place-spirits.

**Money and records.** Chit, docket, stub, tab, till, ledger, tally, script,
scrip, claim, remit, receipt.

**Places.** Street suffixes, transit stops, lighthouses, named winds, islands,
dams, observatories.

**One collection, emptied.** The professional move: gather every noun one domain
uses for a single idea. Small things from tiny cars, microprocessors, seeds, and
stitches in one list. The resonance lives in the collection.

## How to work a list

1. Read it aloud, twice.
2. Write every word that made you pause. Twenty words minimum.
3. For each paused word, write two mutations: how it sounds when a tired person
   says it, and one letter changed that does not look like a typo.
4. Read the survivors against the top-rung sentence and keep the ones that fit.

Worked chain, for a fridge app whose top rung is "nothing dies in my fridge":

```text
List: kitchen and trades
Paused: larder, cure, crock, render, proof, keep
Felt benefit: keeping, rescuing, second life
Adjacent list: water and shipping, so stow, hold, bilge, draught
Into the naming pass: Stow, Hold, Bilge, Larder, Crock
Finalist: Stow (concrete, a verb, one syllable, no collision in the space)
```

No candidate contains `food`, `fresh`, `waste`, or `save`, and none of them is a
virtue with a suffix. That is the whole point of this file.
