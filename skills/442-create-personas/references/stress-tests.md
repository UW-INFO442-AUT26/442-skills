# Four tests that find a bad cast

Run these over the whole set, not one persona at a time. Each failure is a
question for the student, not a silent rewrite: the cast is theirs.

## 1. Swap test

Swap two personas' quotes. If the quotes still fit, they are one persona
written twice.

Ask: "These two could trade quotes. Do we merge them, or is one of them
missing the thing that makes them different?"

## 2. Receipt test

Every load-bearing claim has a concrete detail: a tool, a time, a number,
a habit, a name.

Ask: "What is the detail behind 'busy'? A day, a shift, a number of
interruptions?"

A claim with no detail is a placeholder. Either find the detail or mark it
`assumed`.

## 3. Tension test

Each persona names what they would trade away and what they would never
trade. A persona with no tension is a costume: it agrees with whatever the
team already wanted.

Ask: "What would Priya give up for speed, and what would she refuse to
give up even then?"

## 4. Decision test

Each persona changes at least one design decision, and no two change the
same decision in the same direction. Two personas pulling the same way are
one persona plus noise.

Ask: "Which decision does this one change that the others do not?"

## Then the paragraph

Write one short paragraph: what this cast forces the product to give up.
If the cast can all be satisfied at once, it is not a cast, it is a
mailing list, and the product will be vague in exactly the places these
people should have made it sharp.

The paragraph is the deliverable that proves the cast is doing work. Put
it at the top of `PERSONAS.md`, under the "why these personas" block.
